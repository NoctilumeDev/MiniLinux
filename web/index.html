MiniLinux
A teaching kernel, written primarily in C. Linux-inspired, independently implemented.
The browser forwards commands to a real user-mode shell in QEMU. Observations come from its second serial port. Displays describe the latest recorded event; they are not an instantaneous CPU probe.
